Laser Care Guide

Everything You Need to Know About At-Home Laser Hair Reduction

Dr. Priya Sharma (MD Dermatologist)
Published September 2026 • 5 min read

Laser hair reduction has long been the gold standard for achieving silky, smooth skin without the pain of waxing or frequent shaving. But today, you no longer need to travel to a clinic or spend time in waiting rooms.

How Does Home Laser Hair Reduction Work?

At Smooth N Shine, our clinical specialists arrive at your home equipped with compact, hospital-grade triple-wavelength diode lasers (755nm + 808nm + 1064nm). These portable units feature contact ice-cooling technology to ensure your skin stays comfortable throughout the session.

Is it Safe for Home Environments?

Yes! Safety is our highest priority. Our medical therapists follow strict sanitation protocols:

  • Protection goggles provided to both client and therapist.
  • Single-use disposable sheets for your couch or bed.
  • Antiseptic skin cleansing prior to laser pass.
  • Post-laser soothing aloe vera and SPF application.
"With 6 to 8 sessions spaced 4 weeks apart, most clients achieve up to 90% permanent hair reduction with zero disruption to their daily routine."

Pre-Care Guidelines

Shave the target area 12-24 hours prior to your scheduled home visit. Avoid waxing, threading, or plucking for at least 3 weeks before the laser session, as the laser energy needs active hair follicles to absorb light energy.
